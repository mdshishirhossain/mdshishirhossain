# Nirapod — Research Survey Application

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

**Research software · Department of Computer Science and Engineering, NPI University of Bangladesh**

## Overview

Nirapod is a Bangla-language **research survey application** concerning women's experiences accessing washrooms outside the home. It is a data-collection instrument for a departmental study, **not** a completed public washroom-finder application.

The system supports consent-based participation, structured responses, and tools for authorized research supervision.

## Features and engineering work

- Bangla-language participant survey with structured questions and consent handling.
- Offline draft support and mechanisms to resume a survey.
- Supervisor interface for monitoring fieldwork and managing study activity.
- Export tools for research analysis.
- Controlled handling of participant contact details and access to study data.
- Application checks and tests for questionnaire logic and data workflows.

## Technology

**Next.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Prisma**

The repository also includes deployment and backup scripts designed for a Linux VPS, PostgreSQL database, and Nginx-based hosting setup.

## Project access and confidentiality

**Status:** Deployed for ongoing research data collection.

The development repository is **private**. This public description intentionally does not expose participant answers, contact information, survey access tokens, internal staff routes, credentials, database backups, or infrastructure access details.

A public demonstration should use dummy data and an independent environment, and only be published after appropriate research-supervisor approval. The ongoing survey environment is **not** a public demo.
